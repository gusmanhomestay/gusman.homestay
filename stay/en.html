🏠 Stay Guide ⌄
  • No smoking inside the house (living room, bedrooms, bathroom, and toilet). Smoking is allowed only on the back patio and front terrace.
  • Please keep noise to a minimum and respect the surrounding neighborhood.
  • Keep the house and all facilities clean.
  • Turn off lights, gas, and electronic devices when leaving the house.
  • Used towels/bed sheets/blankets: please place them in the designated area (near the washing machine).
  • Toilet/bidet water pressure: slightly open the sink tap first, then use the bidet slowly to keep water pressure stronger / stable.
  • Please do not leave valuables unattended. Loss due to guest negligence is the guest’s responsibility.
  • If there is any damage or missing item, please report it immediately to the PIC.
  • Amenities are for use during the stay only (items allowed to take home: guest slippers, toothbrush & toothpaste).
  • Smart TV & streaming: use the “Gusman Homestay”/“Homestay” profile and do not change account settings.
